Methodology

Hardware: Intel Core i7-10610U, 4 cores / 8 threads; 32 GB DDR4 at 2667 MT/s. CPU capped at 1.8 GHz, performance governor, turbo disabled. Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, Linux 7.0.0-28.

All proxies ran on the same machine: four workers on two physical cores, with one core each for the generator and backend, over loopback. Each scenario ran for 10 seconds, repeated three times in interleaved order. Routing, header handling and upstream keep-alive were configured for equivalent work; access logging was off.

EdgeRush revision ff377cd; NGINX 1.28.3; HAProxy 3.2.9. These results predate access logs and measure small-response throughput, not production capacity. Read the harness and configs.
